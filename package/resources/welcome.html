Teams Bridge

Control Microsoft Teams from your Stream Deck, mute yourself automatically after silence, and report your call status to Homey.

This installs:

  • Teams Bridge in Applications — a small menu bar app that runs in the background and starts at login.
  • The Stream Deck plugin — Stream Deck will ask you to confirm adding it.

Requires macOS 13 or later. Works on Apple silicon and Intel. If you're upgrading from an earlier install, it's replaced automatically and your settings are kept.
